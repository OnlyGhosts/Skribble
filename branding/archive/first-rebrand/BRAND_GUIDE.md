# Brand guide

## Approved direction
Dark, confident, friendly social gaming. Bold lowercase typographic wordmark `boredgames.io`, a quieter `.io`, simple phone characters and a restrained lime/lilac palette. Keep the designer feel through alignment, spacing and purposeful contrast.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Page | #141514 |
| Panel | #20221F |

Use dark backgrounds for the default theme. Lime is an accent, not a page background. Primary buttons have ink text. Use lilac sparingly in illustration and headline emphasis. Do not use accent text for long body copy.

## Typography and wordmark
Use a heavy compact grotesk for logo and headings; clean regular sans for body; monospace only for room codes and small labels. Reuse a suitable installed project font if available. System fallback: Arial Black / Arial Bold for display and Arial for body. Do not install or invent a named custom font from the AI reference. Desktop h1 64–80px, mobile 40–48px; line height 1.02; letter spacing approximately -0.04em. Body 16–18px with 1.5 line height; labels 11–12px.

Always write boredgames.io as one lowercase word. Keep clear space at least one lowercase letter height; retain a legible minimum width. No controller icon alongside primary wordmark. Use the smile tile for the small favicon/avatar.

## Characters
Lilac: bored, flat eyes and short flat mouth. Lime: happy, curved eyes and smile. Tall rounded phone silhouettes, small speaker slot, arms and feet, solid dark outline and hard offset shadow. Flat uninterrupted colour behind the faces. No screen border, glass lines, bevel, shine, reflection or gradients. SVGs supplied with transparent backgrounds. Characters support the product, they never compete with the room form.

## Voice
Short, sociable, gently cheeky. Approved copy: “Less bored. More games.”, “Boredom has left the chat.”, “Get your people together.”, “Made for one more round.” Avoid corporate filler, fake usage statistics or unverified product claims.

## Avoid
Glassmorphism, neon glow, gradients, generic controller badges, excessive pills, sparkles, oversized decorative scenes and glossy 3D mascots. Use fine borders, modest corners and flat surfaces. Motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
