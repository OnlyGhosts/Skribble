# Brand

- [`pocket-party/`](pocket-party/START_HERE.md): the Pocket Party pack, the brand and UI authority (approved board, specs, tokens, icons, mascots, app icon, offline preview).
- [`mascots/`](mascots/boredgames-mascots.svg): the two phone characters as the owner drew them in Illustrator, the master file. The site draws the same shapes in `client/src/platform/components/Mascots.tsx`, and a unit test checks they match.
- [`logo/`](logo/): the boredgames.io wordmark outlined in Poppins Black ("boredgames") and Poppins Regular (".io"), the owner's pick (dark, light, ink, off-white, one-colour and two-tone `currentColor` versions).
- [`archive/first-rebrand/`](archive/first-rebrand/README.md): the previous pack and logo set, superseded.

The owner's decisions override the pack where they differ: content modes are Family and Unhinged (18+) only, there is no max-players setting, the drawing game is called Scribble (internal id `skribble`), sound effects are wanted, Blackjack and Poker get new flows, and no user-facing text uses em dashes or taglines. The pack also shows Hangman and Battleships, which are no longer on the site.
